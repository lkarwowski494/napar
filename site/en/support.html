Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings and choose Export Backup (JSON). You will get a complete, readable JSON file containing your recipes, bean & tea pantry, and brew journal, which you can save to Files or archive anywhere you like.

What happens if I switch phones?

Before switching devices, export a JSON backup on your current phone. On your new phone, install Napar, open Settings → Import JSON Backup, and load your backup file to restore all your records locally.

Does the app use mobile data or cloud servers?

No. Napar is 100% offline-first and makes no background network connections. Your entire database and all calculators run locally on your device.

How does automatic data-loss protection work?

Before every import or database reset, Napar creates a safety snapshot in its local Backup/ directory. If an imported file is damaged or invalid, your existing data stays intact.

Is the app paid?

No. Napar v1.0 is completely free with no in-app purchases, no ads, and no paywalls; every feature is available from first launch.
